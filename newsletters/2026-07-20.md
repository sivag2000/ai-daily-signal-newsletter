# Agentic AI Ascendant
**Issue #055 · July 20, 2026**
*As frontier models unlock new agency, market dynamics and safety concerns intensify.*

## TL;DR
- OpenAI launched **GPT-5.6**, now powering Microsoft 365 Copilot and new agentic ChatGPT Work.
- Databricks' valuation soared to **$188 billion**, cementing its leadership in the AI data ecosystem.
- NVIDIA’s Jensen Huang secured broad tech deals across Japan, expanding global AI infrastructure.

## THE BIG STORY
OpenAI has ushered in a new era of "agentic AI" with the release of **GPT-5.6**, now the preferred model in Microsoft 365 Copilot. This update marks a significant leap in intelligence and efficiency, promising stronger performance per dollar and enhanced capability for complex enterprise tasks. Crucially, OpenAI also introduced ChatGPT Work—an autonomous agent designed to take action across various applications and files, capable of staying with a project for hours to transform a goal into finished work.

This advancement profoundly matters for how businesses will leverage AI. The shift towards agentic capabilities means AI is moving beyond mere assistance to becoming a proactive partner, managing multi-step workflows with greater autonomy. Enterprises can now measure ROI more effectively by focusing on useful work performed, cost per successful task, and dependability, as highlighted by OpenAI's CFO Sarah Friar. This push towards "agentic" AI is directly tied to managing AI investments by improving efficiency and scaling high-value workflows.

The immediate impact will be felt across organizations utilizing Microsoft 365 Copilot, which now benefits from GPT-5.6's enhanced capabilities in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Chat. Developers also gain access to more powerful tools for building sophisticated, reliable AI agents. While offering unprecedented productivity gains, this also raises the stakes for robust AI safety and alignment, as these agents increasingly operate with minimal human intervention.

## MORE SIGNALS

### Databricks Hits Massive Valuation
Databricks has achieved a remarkable **$188 billion** valuation, solidifying its position as a central player in the AI ecosystem. The company has successfully remade its image as a leading AI data platform, moving beyond its data warehousing origins. Furthermore, Databricks published research demonstrating the significant cost savings achievable through the use of open-weight AI models for coding, reinforcing its value proposition for developers and enterprises seeking efficient AI solutions.

### NVIDIA Forges Japan Alliances
NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang concluded a high-profile visit to Tokyo, securing widespread deals across Japan's technology ecosystem. These collaborations span multiple sectors, underscoring NVIDIA's expanding role as a foundational provider of AI computing power and platforms globally. The move further integrates NVIDIA's hardware and software solutions into key international markets, paving the way for advanced AI development and deployment across diverse industries.

### China's Kimi Model Draws Scrutiny
Chinese AI firm Moonshot AI released an updated version of its **Kimi** model this week, generating significant discussion and some concern within the tech community. Commentators, including some on the "AI Revolution" YouTube channel, highlighted the model's advanced capabilities. The release prompted specific worries from some observers regarding "full AI communism," suggesting a geopolitical dimension to the rapid advancements in AI models from China.
