# Superintelligence: A Reckoning
**Issue #106 · September 09, 2026**
*As AI capabilities soar, so do the urgent calls for caution and control.*

## TL;DR
-   Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon resigned, warning against self-improving AI and urging pacing agreements among labs.
-   Autonomous AI agents like Instinct and Meta's Muse are rapidly expanding their capabilities across daily tasks and enterprise.
-   OpenAI's claim of an AI-generated solution to the Navier–Stokes problem sparks both excitement and academic controversy.

## THE BIG STORY
AI safety concerns reached a fever pitch this week as Anthropic researcher **Jacob Coxon** resigned, publicly warning against the dangers of self-improving AI and "gambling with our lives." Coxon's departure underscores a growing internal struggle within leading AI labs over the rapid pursuit of advanced general intelligence, with his call for industry-wide pacing agreements highlighting a deep rift on responsible development. This internal dissent amplifies the external warnings from experts and policymakers alike.

**Connor Leahy** of ControlAI emphasized that superintelligence is "not a weapon, it’s an adversary," advocating for robust regulation to prevent catastrophic outcomes. Senator **Bernie Sanders** echoed these sentiments, delivering a stark warning about rapidly developing AI and the potential for a "first-of-its-kind A.I. cyberattack against a government," as reportedly disclosed by Israeli firm Dream. Such alarms suggest that the race for superintelligence may be pushing past current safety mechanisms, affecting not just developers but potentially global stability and the very fabric of society. Executives and leaders in the AI space face increasing pressure to balance innovation with stringent safety protocols.

## MORE SIGNALS
### Agentic AI Everywhere
The era of autonomous AI agents is accelerating, moving beyond simple chatbots to performing complex, multi-step tasks. **Instinct**, a viral AI assistant, now boasts its own email address, enabling it to create accounts, contact businesses, and handle support on users' behalf. Simultaneously, **Meta** launched **Muse**, a personal AI agent designed to manage everyday tasks, sell cars, or book flights, directly competing with services like OpenClaw. In the retail sector, **Instacart** introduced **Clementine** and **Shipt** rolled out its own AI shopping assistant, allowing users to build complex grocery lists and meal plans via conversational commands. This expansion signifies a major shift towards AI systems proactively executing tasks, fundamentally changing human-computer interaction across consumer and enterprise applications.

### OpenAI’s Mathematical Feat and Controversy
**OpenAI** announced an AI-generated solution to the **Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize Problem**, including a formal proof in Lean. This potential breakthrough, if validated, would represent a significant advancement in complex mathematical problem-solving by AI. However, the announcement was quickly overshadowed by accusations of impropriety from some academics, raising questions about the rigor and transparency of AI-driven scientific discovery. Separately, OpenAI continued to enhance its creative suite, introducing **ChatGPT Images 2.5**, which allows for more personalized and polished image generation from sketches and ideas.

### AI Spending Shifts
Recent data indicates a slump in AI spend per employee at top firms during August, raising questions about whether this is merely a "summer doldrums" effect or a more concerning signal for the industry. Factors cited include falling token costs and cheaper models, suggesting that while AI adoption continues, the economic dynamics—especially for hyperscalers—might be evolving differently than anticipated. This trend could influence investment strategies and the valuation of AI-centric companies in the coming months, prompting a closer look at efficiency versus raw spending.
