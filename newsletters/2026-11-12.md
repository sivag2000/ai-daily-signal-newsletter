# AI Leadership in Flux
**Issue #170 · November 12, 2026**
*Geopolitical shifts, AI's market impact, and the evolving landscape of innovation.*

## TL;DR
- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned amid controversy, tanking **$470 billion** in market value and casting doubt on open-source AI commitments.
- Google rolled out new Gemini 4 models, **Hydrogen** for agentic workflows and **Carbon** for advanced complex coding and agent teams.
- SoftBank incurred a **$3 billion** loss on natural gas turbines for AI data centers, signaling potential limits to energy-intensive AI expansion.

## THE BIG STORY
The AI industry was rocked this week by the resignation of Nvidia co-founder and CEO Jensen Huang, following an internal investigation into an alleged affair with an individual suspected of spying for China. Huang's departure sent shockwaves through Nvidia, resulting in an estimated **$470 billion**—or over **$1 trillion** in less than a month—loss in market value for the chipmaking giant, according to Wired. The scandal not only casts a long shadow over Nvidia's market dominance but also raises critical questions about the company's future direction and its significant influence on the broader AI ecosystem.

Huang's leadership was instrumental in positioning Nvidia as the undeniable leader in AI hardware, shaping key industry trends, and fostering open-source AI initiatives, including its **$12.9 billion** acquisition of Hugging Face. With his exit, the future of these commitments is now uncertain, prompting Hugging Face's CEO to publicly vow continued advocacy for open-source AI despite the leadership vacuum. The incident underscores the deep entanglement of geopolitical concerns with corporate leadership, particularly in sensitive sectors like advanced AI technology.

The sudden change affects everyone from developers relying on Nvidia's open-weight models to investors holding Nvidia stock, and even policymakers grappling with technology security. Huang has agreed to testify before Congress, hinting at further scrutiny to come. This event signals a potentially transformative moment for Nvidia and the industry, forcing a re-evaluation of its strategies and partnerships as it navigates this unprecedented leadership transition. Former President Trump has already publicly suggested Apple chairman Tim Cook as a potential successor.

## MORE SIGNALS
### Google's Gemini Ecosystem Expands
Google is advancing its AI capabilities with the release of two new models. **Gemini 4 Hydrogen** focuses on fast, cost-efficient agentic workflows and everyday tasks, now integrated into Google Search's AI Mode for better understanding of complex queries and richer visual responses. Simultaneously, **Gemini 4 Carbon**—touted as Google's most powerful model yet—is now more widely accessible for tackling complex coding and powering advanced agent teams.

### Anthropic Files for IPO
AI safety-focused startup Anthropic has officially filed to become a public company, offering a significant opportunity for investors to engage with a major player in the competitive large language model (LLM) market. The move signals a maturation for the company behind the Claude Fable 5.2 model, positioning it for further expansion and capital investment in AI research and development.

### SoftBank's AI Energy Ambitions Hit Snag
SoftBank suffered a substantial **$3 billion** loss after preordering natural gas turbines intended to power AI data centers. This significant financial setback calls into question the long-term economic viability and scale of energy-intensive AI infrastructure, potentially placing a cap on the industry's rapid expansion if sustainable and affordable power solutions aren't swiftly developed.
